| Helland (2012) | Replays acknowledge the stored fact; a mutated payload is a conflict, not an update. |
| IEEE 754-2019 | Decimal interchange stays in the decimal domain; binary inexact types are rejected. |
| ISO 8601-1:2019 | `occurred_at` and usage windows are timezone-aware instants; naive timestamps fail closed. |
| FOCUS 1.4 | Cost, invoice, billing-period, and commitment data are export projections, not the internal operational schema. |
| IFRS 15 | Billing and revenue recognition remain separate; an invoice-intent draft, issued commercial invoice snapshot, issued commercial credit-note snapshot, journal proposal, collection reminder, commercial credit adjustment, invoice-draft presentment statement, and collection-case presentment statement are consideration evidence, not proof that revenue has been earned or reversed. The operator Storybook console presents those same exact-decimal amounts, including billing-account remaining and unused-void totals, and the next action (collect, credit, park, apply, or refund) without claiming earned revenue. A later price concession or billing correction is variable consideration. Amounts collected on behalf of a tax authority are not usage revenue; Billing credits semantic `tax_payable` on the invoice journal and debits it on a taxed credit or taxed unused-invoice void so AIS books do not drift. Performance-obligation and principal-versus-agent judgments belong to versioned accounting policy in AIS. |
| IFRS 18 | The Accounting Information Platform owns presentation categories, required subtotals, management-defined performance measure disclosures, and the 2027 transition; Billing does not encode statement presentation when it emits a journal proposal. |
| IFRS Accounting Taxonomy 2025 | Financial-report output remains taxonomy-versioned, with the 2025 taxonomy retained for 2026 reporting. |
| IAS 7 | Bank and settlement facts are classified and presented by Accounting rather than inferred from a payment-provider status alone. Billing proposes semantic `cash_receipt` versus `accounts_receivable` roles, semantic `write_off_expense` versus `accounts_receivable` for leftover remaining, semantic `unapplied_cash` versus `cash_receipt` for leftover refunds, semantic `cash_receipt` versus `unapplied_cash` for parked leftover, and semantic `unapplied_cash` versus `accounts_receivable` for leftover apply; AIS owns the cash-flow and impairment classification. |
| IAS 21 | Transaction, functional, and presentation currencies are separate accounting concepts; Billing preserves source currency and amounts. |
| ISO 20022-1:2026 | Bank and treasury adapters use versioned financial-message mappings rather than proprietary fields in the accounting core. Payment initiation stays a provider-neutral `payment_intent` until a later adapter projects an ISO 20022 initiation message (`pain.001`). Commercial settlement is a `payment_receipt` applied against that intent; the cash journal proposal is billing evidence for AIS, not an ISO 20022 `camt` settlement or clearing message. A commercial `credit_adjustment` is billing evidence for a credit note, not a posted ISO 20022 credit-note settlement. A commercial `issued_credit_note` is a customer-facing credit-note snapshot, not a statutory credit note or ISO 20022 credit-note settlement. Invoice-draft presentment is a commercial statement, not a posted ISO 20022 invoice message. AIS pulls journal evidence and later returns a posting receipt; Billing stores the receipt as an observation and does not post it. |
