# Metering Billing Platform Development Context

This repository is the CWL commercial usage and billing authority. It is provider-neutral and accounting-aware, but it is not the statutory accounting authority.

Before changing behavior:

1. Identify the authoritative fact being changed.
2. Add a failing test for monetary and idempotency behavior.
3. Preserve immutable history through correction or reversal records.
4. Keep provider integration behind capability-specific ports.
5. Export accounting proposals without claiming legal posting.
6. Ingest usage through `metering_billing.UsageIngestionService` so retries are idempotent and tenants cannot attribute usage to each other.
7. Rate stored usage through `metering_billing.UsageRatingService` so a tenant window produces exact invoice-intent totals from billable quality only.
8. Draft invoice intent through `metering_billing.InvoiceDraftService` from a stored rating run.  Do not issue, collect, or post from that path.
9. Export a journal proposal through `metering_billing.AccountingExportService` from a stored invoice draft, or a cash journal from a stored payment receipt.  Hand the proposal to AIS.  Do not post journals, open fiscal periods, or resolve statutory account IDs.
10. Open a collection case through `metering_billing.CollectionCaseService` from a stored invoice draft.  Send a dunning notice next.  Do not capture payment or post journals from that path.
11. Project a payment intent through `metering_billing.PaymentIntentService` from a stored collection case.  Bind a provider projection later or cancel the intent.  Do not capture, settle, or post from that path.
12. Record a payment receipt through `metering_billing.PaymentSettlementService` from a stored projected payment intent.  Apply the exact received amount, reduce collection outstanding, settle the case when remaining is zero, and idempotently propose the existing cash journal.  Do not capture via a provider or post journals.
13. Propose a cash journal through `metering_billing.AccountingExportService.propose_cash_journal` from a stored payment receipt.  Receipt accept already composes this write; the HTTP path remains a manual replay.  Hand the persisted cash proposal to AIS.  Do not change collection outstanding, capture via a provider, or post journals.
14. Accept the same writes over HTTP through `metering_billing.http_app.create_http_app` or `python -m metering_billing.http_app`.  Require a tenant on every write.  Return each service `as_contract_dict` result as JSON.  Do not post journals, store a card PAN, or add a named payment provider.
15. Let AIS pull persisted journal proposals through `GET /v1/journal-proposals` and `GET /v1/journal-proposals/{proposal_id}`.  Require a tenant on every read.  Accept optional `X-CWL-Tenant-Reference`; body or query `tenant_reference` still works when the header is absent; a mismatch is 422.  Do not mark exported, posted, or consumed.  Do not emit statutory account IDs.  AIS pulls validated proposals and owns `posting_receipt`.
16. Pull an AIS posting receipt through `metering_billing.PostingReceiptPullService.pull_posting_receipt` after AIS accept.  Store it as a commercial observation.  If AIS returns 404, accept the proposal on AIS and retry.  Do not flip Billing `proposal_status`.  Do not start credit or adjustment from that path.
17. Record a commercial credit through `metering_billing.CreditAdjustmentService.record_credit_adjustment` from a stored invoice draft.  Apply the exact inclusive credit amount, reduce collection outstanding when a case exists, and emit one validated journal proposal.  When the draft is taxed, split the credit proportionally and debit `usage_revenue` plus `tax_payable` against an `accounts_receivable` credit.  Untaxed credits stay two-line revenue/AR.  Record the credit; AIS pulls the validated three-line unwind.  Do not post, call AIS, or open a fiscal period from that path.
18. Publish a tenant-scoped rate card through `metering_billing.RateCardService.publish_rate_card`.  Persist one `rate_card` and one immutable `rate_card_version` with `rate_card_line` rows.  Rate stored usage only against that persisted version.  A missing or cross-tenant version, or a missing metric on the card, fails closed.  Do not invent a hidden default price or edit a published version from that path.  Publish a rate card, then rate a window against that version.
19. Publish a tenant-scoped tax rate through `metering_billing.TaxRateService.publish_tax_rate` and assess a stored draft through `metering_billing.TaxAssessmentService.assess_tax`.  Persist one `tax_rate_schedule`, one immutable `tax_rate_version`, and one `tax_assessment`.  Round tax half-even to the documented ISO 4217 minor units.  Reject assess after collection is open.  Propose a three-line journal when assessed (`accounts_receivable`, `usage_revenue`, `tax_payable`).  AIS must map `tax_payable`.  Do not call an OSS tax engine, store exemptions, or start operator UI from that path.  Publish a tax rate, assess the draft, then propose the journal and let AIS pull.
20. Present a stored draft through `metering_billing.InvoicePresentmentService.present_invoice_draft`.  `GET /v1/invoice-drafts/{invoice_draft_id}` returns the tenant-scoped statement.  Cross-tenant or unknown is 404.  Open the draft statement, then collect or credit.  Do not post, call AIS, or start a web UI from that path.
21. Issue a tenant API credential through `metering_billing.TenantApiCredentialService.issue_credential`.  Return prefix plus secret once and persist only a keyed HMAC.  After one active key exists, every `/v1` call except credential issue requires that key.  Zero active keys keep the tenant pin so AIS can keep pulling with `X-CWL-Tenant-Reference`.  `GET /healthz` stays unauthenticated.  Issue a key, then send it on every `/v1` call; revoke when leaked.  Do not log the secret, put it on AIS contracts, or start a web UI from that path.
22. Render a stored presentment statement in `operator_console` Storybook.  Use design tokens and the InvoiceStatement, AmountDue, LineTable, and StatusChip stories.  Show amount due and the next operator action: collect or credit.  Do not replace the Python package, add a production SPA, call AIS, or add Stripe.
23. Register an https webhook callback through `metering_billing.WebhookSubscriptionService.register_subscription`, then run `WebhookDeliveryService.deliver_due_events` or `POST /v1/webhook-deliveries`.  Accepted journal proposals, payment receipts, and credits append `webhook_outbox_event` rows.  Sign POSTs with `X-CWL-Webhook-Signature: sha256=<hex>`.  AIS may keep polling.  Do not flip `proposal_status`, call AIS posting-receipt, or emit statutory IDs.
24. Drain AIS `posting_receipt` outbox events through `metering_billing.AisOutboxDrainService.drain_ais_outbox` or `POST /v1/ais-outbox-drains`.  Match constructed `urn:cwl:accounting:posting_receipt:{proposal_id}` and `urn:cwl:accounting:general_journal:{proposal_id}` by equality.  Do not parse the payload URN.  GET `/posting-receipts` with the stored Billing `idempotency_key` only.  Empty unpublished pages skip receipt GETs.  After a stored observation, POST publish.  Do not drain `journal_reversal` or `period_close`.  Do not flip `proposal_status`.
25. Present a stored collection case through `metering_billing.CollectionCasePresentmentService.present_collection_case`.  `GET /v1/collection-cases/{collection_case_id}` returns the tenant-scoped statement.  `GET /v1/collection-cases` lists `{collection_cases, next_cursor}`.  Cross-tenant or unknown is 404.  Open the collection case, then collect or credit.  Do not capture, call AIS, flip `proposal_status`, or start a production SPA.
26. Project and present a stored payment intent through `metering_billing.PaymentIntentService.project_payment_intent` and `PaymentIntentPresentmentService.present_payment_intent`.  `POST /v1/payment-intents` keys on `collection_case_id`.  `GET /v1/payment-intents/{payment_intent_id}` returns the tenant-scoped statement.  `GET /v1/payment-intents` lists `{payment_intents, next_cursor}`.  Cross-tenant or unknown is 404.  Create a projected payment intent, then record the receipt.  Do not capture, call AIS, flip `proposal_status`, or start a production SPA.
27. Apply and present a stored payment receipt through `metering_billing.PaymentSettlementService.record_payment_receipt` and `PaymentReceiptPresentmentService.present_payment_receipt`.  `POST /v1/payment-receipts` keys on `payment_intent_id` and composes the #13 cash journal.  `GET /v1/payment-receipts/{payment_receipt_id}` returns the tenant-scoped statement.  `GET /v1/payment-receipts` lists `{payment_receipts, next_cursor}`.  Cross-tenant or unknown is 404.  Record the receipt; the cash journal is already validated for AIS to pull.  Do not capture, call AIS, flip `proposal_status`, or start a production SPA.
